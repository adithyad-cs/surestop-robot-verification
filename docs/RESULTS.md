## Metric definitions

- **falseArrivalRate**: Trials that ended ARRIVED at a candidate that is not a 3-D potted plant ÷ trials in which at least one non-target candidate was opened (encountered).
- **correctConfirmationRate**: Trials that ended ARRIVED at the 3-D potted plant ÷ trials in which a 3-D potted plant is present.
- **abstentionRate**: Trials that ended UNRESOLVED (an engaged candidate stayed UNCONFIRMED) ÷ trials in which at least one candidate was opened.
- **completionRate**: Trials with the correct terminal outcome (ARRIVED at the target if present; NOT_FOUND if absent) ÷ all trials (excluding aborted).
